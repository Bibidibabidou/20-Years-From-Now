Act 02 · Naming it

Nobody told this page to do that.

That is the question of this story. Since June 2026, the Americans on AI survey has asked about 1,000 US adults every two weeks how likely it is that, in the next 20 years, AI systems will behave in ways their own developers did not intend.

How we know

Question
“Thinking about the next 20 years, how likely is each of the following to happen? AI systems behaving in ways their developers did not intend.” Futures block, original wording, Release 008 p. 45.
Source
Americans on AI by Athena Insights, fielded by NORC at the University of Chicago on the AmeriSpeak probability panel.
Waves
8 waves from 25 June to 8 October 2026, about 1,000 US adults each, weighted. The June pilot used a different panel and is excluded.
Margin of error
±4.1 points per wave for all adults. Wider for party, age and gender groups.
Dots
1 dot = 0.1% of US adults. The ring a dot sits in is its answer group. Its exact position inside the ring carries no data.
“Unlikely or not sure”
Unlikely, not at all likely, not sure and no answer, shown together as one outer belt.
Agency
8 Oct 2026: 67% “AI is coming into our lives whether we want it or not”, 16% “People like me can shape how much AI is part of our lives”.
Visitors
Visitor answers in this prototype are demo data. In the final version they are anonymous counts, labelled as not representative and never merged with the survey.
Not shown
Why attitudes moved. The survey measures perception, not what AI systems actually do.
